{# --- FEATURED ADULTS DOUBLING --- #}

Featured Adults — Easy Doubles

Most Featured Adults appear in only one or two scenes. They can safely double as ensemble in other scenes. Grouped by when they appear:

{# Danielle & Louis #}

Danielle & Louis

Appear in: What You Got only
Safe doubles
  • Students, Parents, Wedding Guests, Company
  • Any ensemble section except What You Got
{# School Faculty #}

Dr. Ehrin / Señor O'Brien / Mrs. Luckenbill

Appear in: Somebody Has Got to Take the Blame
Safe doubles
  • Parents, Wedding Guests, Company
  • Catering Staff (different scene)
Watch out
  • Teachers (same actors may be recognizable)
{# Ms. Meyers #}

Ms. Meyers

Appear in: Watch Your Back only
Safe doubles
  • Parents, Wedding Guests, Company, Catering Staff
{# Officers #}

Officer Sitz & Officer Kowalski

Appear in: Bring My Baby Home (Act II) only
Safe doubles
  • Students, Teachers, Parents (Act I scenes)
  • Catering Staff, Wedding Guests
  • Any Act I ensemble role
{# Mrs. Time #}

Mrs. Time

Appear in: No More Fear (Act II) only
Safe doubles
  • Any Act I Featured Adult role (Danielle, Louis, etc.)
  • Parents, Wedding Guests, Catering Staff
{# Pastor Bruno #}

Pastor Bruno

Appear in: Today and Ev'ry Day (finale) only
Safe doubles
  • Any Act I or early Act II Featured Adult
  • Teachers, Catering Staff
{# Grandparents #}

Grandpa Gordon & Grandma Helen

Appear in: Scene work / wedding only
Safe doubles
  • Teachers, Catering Staff
  • Any non-wedding ensemble scene
{# --- ENSEMBLE GROUPS --- #}

Ensemble Groups — Overlap Chart

Ensemble bucket roles and where they can safely share performers.

{# Students #}

Students

Appear in: Multiple Act I & II numbers Load: Heavy
Safe doubles
  • Wedding Guests (different context/costume)
  • Catering Staff (if not in kitchen scene)
Avoid
  • Teachers, Parents (would break the age split)
{# Teachers #}

Teachers

Appear in: I Got This sections Load: Light
Safe doubles
  • Parents, Wedding Guests, Catering Staff
  • Company (non-school scenes)
{# Parents #}

Parents

Appear in: Busted, Just One Day Reprise, Not Myself Today, Finale Load: Medium
Safe doubles
  • Teachers, Catering Staff
  • Wedding Guests (natural overlap — many parents attend the wedding)
{# Catering Staff #}

Catering Staff

Appear in: What You Got only Load: Very light
Safe doubles
  • Students, Teachers, Parents, Wedding Guests
  • Essentially any other ensemble group
{# Savannah's Minions #}

Savannah's Minions

Appear in: Go (Act II) only Load: Very light
Safe doubles
  • Students (natural fit — they ARE students)
  • Wedding Guests, Company
{# Wedding Guests #}

Wedding Guests

Appear in: Just One Day Reprise, Not Myself Today, Finale Load: Medium
Safe doubles
  • Students (costume change between school/wedding)
  • Parents (many parents are also guests)
  • Catering Staff, Teachers

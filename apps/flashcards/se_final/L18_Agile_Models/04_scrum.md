**L18.4** *Explain the Scrum model with advantages/disadvantages.*
?
Scrum uses:
- Product backlog
- Sprints (1–4 weeks)
- Sprint planning
- Daily stand-ups
- Sprint review
- Sprint retrospective

**Advantages:**
- Fast adaptation
- Strong team autonomy
- Quick delivery
- Simple structure

**Disadvantages:**
- Requires discipline
- Documentation optional
- Product Owner bottlenecks possible
- Not ideal for huge teams

{# --- ROLE CARDS --- #}

Minor Roles — Cross-Cast Chart

Each card shows where a role can be safely doubled and which combos to avoid.

{# Crows #}

Crows (3 voices)

Range: High trio (S/A) Load: Light Changes: Easy
Ideal doubles
  • Trees, Jitterbug Chorus, Beauticians / Manicurists
  • Ozians, Poppies (Girls), Munchkins (if needed)
Avoid
  • Winkies (opposite vocal colour)
  • Winkie Captain, Emerald City Guard
{# Trees #}

Trees (3 voices)

Range: Middle trio Load: Light Changes: Easy
Ideal doubles
  • Crows, Jitterbug Chorus, Polishers / Manicurists
  • Ozians, Poppies (Girls or Boys)
Avoid
  • Munchkin specialty roles (Tots / Tough Kids)
{# Munchkin Roles #}

Munchkin Trio Roles (multiple)

Range: Mixed Load: Single sequence Changes: Very easy

Includes: Three Tots, Three Tough Kids, Braggart, Mayor, Coroner, Barrister, City Fathers

Ideal doubles
  • Beauticians, Manicurists, Ozians, Jitterbugs
  • Poppies, Polishers, Trees, Crows
Avoid
  • Winkies (conflicts in Act II)
  • Guard (timing clash)
{# Beauticians / Manicurists / Polishers #}

Beauticians / Manicurists / Polishers

Beauticians: High Manicurists: Mid-high Polishers: Mid-low
Ideal doubles
  • Crows, Trees, Jitterbug Chorus
  • Ozians, Poppies, Munchkins
Avoid
  • Winkie Captain, Emerald City Guard
  • Any lead understudy
{# Emerald City Guard #}

Emerald City Guard

Range: Mid baritone / alto Presence: Comedic featured
Ideal doubles
  • Winkie Captain (if strong belt)
  • Ozians, Polishers
Avoid
  • Crows, Trees
  • Munchkin Trio roles (muddies comic clarity)
{# Winkie Captain #}

Winkie Captain

Range: Strong soprano / belt Presence: Featured vocal, Act II
Ideal doubles
  • Emerald City Guard, Ozians
  • Poppies, Jitterbug Chorus
Avoid
  • Trees, Crows, Munchkinland roles
  • (tonal shift: bright Munchkins vs dark Winkies)
{# Jitterbug Chorus #}

Jitterbug Chorus (3-part)

Range: SAT/SSA flexible Energy: High, dance-heavy
Ideal doubles
  • Crows, Trees, Ozians
  • Beauticians, Manicurists
Avoid
  • Guard (timing), Fiddler
  • Featured Munchkin roles
{# Fiddler #}

Fiddler

Range: Mid-high solo (C4–C5) Load: Two tiny features
Ideal doubles
  • Munchkinland roles
  • Beauticians / Manicurists / Polishers, Ozians
Avoid
  • Crows (range mismatch), Winkies

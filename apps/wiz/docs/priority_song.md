Yep, 100%. If harmonies are weak, the whole show sounds like “kids concert” instead of “musical,” no matter how good your solos are.

Let’s re-prioritize with this rule:

Harmony > Everything.
Teach the hardest + most exposed harmony stuff first, then solos and unison numbers get slotted around that.

I’ll group things and then give you a concrete order.

⸻

1. Highest-priority harmony chunks (do these first)

These are the “if these sound bad, the audience will notice immediately” sections.

🔴 Tier 1 – Core ensemble harmony
	1.	Poppies – Girls chorus (4-part harmony)
	•	Number: No. 24 – Poppies (no24_poppies_girls)
	•	Why first:
	•	4-part = maximum brain load for your small ensemble.
	•	Slow enough to really lock tuning and blend.
	•	Great lab for: vowel matching, balance, holding parts.
	2.	Munchkinland – Girls chorus (3-part high)
	•	Number: No. 9 – Munchkinland (no9_munch_girls_3part)
	•	Why now:
	•	High tessitura, thin texture → any wrong note sticks out.
	•	Early in the show, so it has to be solid.
	3.	Jitterbug – 3-part chorus
	•	Number: No. 36 – Jitterbug (no36_jitter_chorus)
	•	Why:
	•	Rhythm + harmony together = hard.
	•	If they can hold harmony at Jitterbug tempo, everything else is easier.
	•	Start slow, as a pure vocal piece before adding groove/dance.
	4.	Merry Old Land of Oz – Beauticians / Manicurists / Polishers
	•	Number: No. 28 – Merry Old Land of Oz
	•	Sections:
	•	Beauticians (no28_merry_beauticians) – high line
	•	Manicurists (no28_merry_manicurists) – also high-ish
	•	Polishers (no28_merry_polishers) – middle
	•	Why:
	•	These are exposed mini-ensembles; if they’re scrappy, the number feels messy.
	•	Also great for testing who can hold a part in a tiny group.

🟠 Tier 2 – Small-group harmonies / trios

Once Tier 1 is underway, start these:
	5.	If I Only Had a Brain – Three Crows harmony
	•	no16_brain_crows_trio
	•	Tricky because:
	•	It’s not “mass” sound – audience hears each wrong interval.
	•	Great for placing your strongest part-holders.
	6.	If I Only Had a Heart – Three Trees interjections
	•	no19_heart_trees_trio
	•	Similar job to Crows: nonsense text + harmony + character.
	7.	Munchkin sequence – Three Tots / Three Tough Kids
	•	no10_munch_three_tots, no10_munch_three_tough_kids
	•	Short but very exposed; kids need time to own their lines.
	8.	Poppies – Round with Dorothy, Scarecrow, Tinman, Lion
	•	no24_poppies_round_quartet
	•	Canon = harmony disguised as “simple”. It will fall apart under stress if not drilled early.
	9.	Jitterbug – Featured Jitterbugs
	•	no36_jitter_featured – often riffy / high energy; they need to be confident over the chorus.

🟡 Tier 3 – Lower lines / octaves & “easier” ensemble colour
	10.	Winkies – Octaves / low unison
	•	no34_winkies_march_octaves, no40_winkies_reprise_march, no41_winkies_with_friends, no43_dingdong_reprise_winkies
	•	Harder than they look:
	•	Low, repetitive, and easy for pitch to sag.
	•	Get them in early enough to build stamina.
	11.	Other ensemble/world bits
	•	Munchkins unison chunks (no10_munch_munchkins_unison, no10_munch_several_unison)
	•	Poppies boys chorus (no24_poppies_boys)
	•	Ozians ensemble (no28_merry_ensemble)

2. Where solos & unison fit around this

Solos are important for the show, but easier to clean late and can be run 1:1 while ensemble does something else.

Priority for solos (once harmony bootcamp is underway)
	1.	Dorothy anchors
	•	Over the Rainbow (No. 4, then reprise no41_otr_reprise_dorothy)
	•	Her lines in Brain/Heart/Nerve and Jitterbug
	2.	Scarecrow / Tinman / Lion
	•	Brain, Heart, Nerve, King of the Forest (no30_king_lion_main)
	3.	Glinda solo (Poppies) + Ding Dong bits
	4.	Guard, Winkie Captain, Coroner, Mayor, Fiddler, Braggart, etc.

These are high-importance dramatically, but rhythmically and pitch-wise they’re simpler than 4-part/3-part ensemble, so you don’t need to front-load them the same way.

3. Concrete rehearsal learning order with harmonies prioritized

Here’s a revised order that respects difficulty:

Phase 1 – Harmony Bootcamp (Ensemble focus)
	1.	Poppies – Girls 4-part harmony (no24_poppies_girls)
	2.	Munchkinland – Girls 3-part (no9_munch_girls_3part)
	3.	Jitterbug – 3-part chorus (no36_jitter_chorus, slow then up to tempo)
	4.	Merry Old Land of Oz – Beauticians / Manicurists / Polishers
	5.	Poppies – Boys chorus (no24_poppies_boys)
	6.	Winkies – March & Reprises (ensemble parts only)
	7.	Ozians ensemble (no28_merry_ensemble, plus any unison/refrain bits)

At this point your 12-person ensemble has done:
	•	high 3-part
	•	4-part
	•	rhythmic harmony
	•	low/octave lines
→ their ears are in shape.

Phase 2 – Small-group harmony & trios
	8.	Crows trio in Brain (no16_brain_crows_trio)
	9.	Trees trio in Heart (no19_heart_trees_trio)
	10.	Three Tots / Three Tough Kids (no10_munch_three_tots, no10_munch_three_tough_kids)
	11.	Poppies round with DSTL (no24_poppies_round_quartet)
	12.	Featured Jitterbugs (no36_jitter_featured)

These also double as audition/cross-cast test pieces for your strong harmony kids.

Phase 3 – World sequences (now that harmony exists)
	13.	Full Munchkin Musical Sequence / Ding Dong
	•	You now drop in the solos (Coroner, Tots, Tough Kids, Mayor, Braggart) on top of harmonies you already taught.
	14.	Full Poppies sequence
	•	Girls/Boys harmony is already there → now add Glinda + quartet round transitions, staging, and acting.
	15.	Full Merry Old Land of Oz + Reprise
	•	Ensemble & mini-groups are pre-taught → add Guard, lead lines, flow.
	16.	Winkies sections with Captain & friends
	•	They already know the chant; now you shape dynamics, entrances, and Captain’s featured lines.
	17.	Jitterbug full number + Reprise
	•	Chorus & featured lines in place → integrate leads, dance, and breath management.

Phase 4 – Solos & journey threads (polish layer)
	18.	Over the Rainbow (main + reprise)
	19.	Brain / Heart / Nerve – solo verses & character work
	20.	We’re Off to See the Wizard – all iterations
	21.	If I Were King of the Forest
	22.	Opening & Entr’acte (tie-in once their harmonic discipline exists)

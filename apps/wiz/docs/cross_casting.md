for small casts (8–14 performers)

This guide tells you:

	•	Who pairs well with who, based on voice type & staging
	•	Which doubling combinations NOT to do

⭐ PART 1 — Quick Reference: Who Can Play What

🌪️ LEAD QUARTET (Dorothy, Scarecrow, Tinman, Lion)

These should NOT be cross-cast into other roles.
They appear too frequently and must stay focused.

⛔ Do NOT double them as:
	•	Trees
	•	Crows
	•	Winkies
	•	Beauticians/Manicurists
	•	Munchkins
	•	Guard
	•	Anything in Jitterbug

⭐ PART 2 — The Minor Roles (Cross-Cast Chart)

Below is the core chart of every small role and exactly where else they can be placed.

⸻

🎤 1. The Crows (3 voices)

Range: High harmony trio (S/A dominated)
Scene load: Light
Quick changes: EASY

✔️ Ideal doubles:
	•	Trees
	•	Jitterbug Chorus
	•	Beauticians / Manicurists
	•	Ozians
	•	Poppies (Girls)
	•	Munchkins (if needed)

✖️ Avoid doubling with:
	•	Winkies (opposite vocal colour)
	•	Winkie Captain
	•	Emerald City Guard

⸻

🌲 2. The Trees (3 voices)

Range: Middle harmony trio
Scene load: Light
Quick changes: EASY

✔️ Ideal doubles:
	•	Crows
	•	Jitterbug Chorus
	•	Polishers / Manicurists
	•	Ozians
	•	Poppies (Girls or Boys depending on voice)

✖️ Avoid doubling with:
	•	Munchkin specialty roles (Tots / Tough Kids)

⸻

🧁 3. The Munchkin Trio Roles
	•	Lullaby League
	•	Lollipop Guild
	•	Three Tots
	•	Three Tough Kids
	•	Braggart
	•	Mayor
	•	Coroner
	•	Barrister
	•	City Fathers

Range: Mixed — some high female, some mid male
Scene load: Single sequence
Quick Changes: VERY EASY

✔️ Ideal doubles:
	•	Beauticians
	•	Manicurists
	•	Ozians
	•	Jitterbugs
	•	Poppies
	•	Polishers
	•	Trees
	•	Crows

✖️ Avoid doubling with:
	•	Winkies (conflicts in Act II)
	•	Guard (timing clash)

⸻

💅 4. Beauticians / Manicurists / Polishers

Range:
	•	Beauticians = high
	•	Manicurists = mid-high
	•	Polishers = mid-low

✔️ Ideal doubles:
	•	Crows
	•	Trees
	•	Jitterbug Chorus
	•	Ozians
	•	Poppies
	•	Munchkins

✖️ Avoid doubling with:
	•	Winkie Captain
	•	Emerald City Guard
	•	Any lead understudy

⸻

🟩 5. Emerald City Guard

Range: Strong singer (mid baritone or alto)
Presence: Comedic featured role

✔️ Ideal doubles:
	•	Winkie Captain (IF strong and female works fine)
	•	Ozians
	•	Polishers

✖️ Avoid doubling with:
	•	Crows
	•	Trees
	•	Munchkin Trio roles

Reason: Guard + Munchkin both need clean comic energy — doubling muddies clarity.

⸻

🟣 6. Winkie Captain

Range: Strong soprano or strong belt (yours is!)
Presence: Featured vocal moment in Act II

✔️ Ideal doubles:
	•	Emerald City Guard
	•	Ozians
	•	Poppies
	•	Jitterbug Chorus

✖️ Avoid doubling with:
	•	Trees
	•	Crows
	•	Munchkinland roles

Because of the tonal shift from bright Munchkins → dark Winkies.

⸻

💃 7. Jitterbug Chorus (3-part)

Range: SAT/SSA flexible
Loads of energy, dancing

✔️ Ideal doubles:
	•	Crows
	•	Trees
	•	Ozians
	•	Beauticians
	•	Manicurists

✖️ Avoid doubling with:
	•	Guard (timing)
	•	Fiddler
	•	Featured Munchkin roles

⸻

🎻 8. Fiddler

Range: Mid-high solo (C4–C5)
Scene load: Two tiny features

✔️ Ideal doubles:
	•	Munchkinland roles
	•	Beauticians / Manicurists / Polishers
	•	Ozians

✖️ Avoid:
	•	Crows (range mismatch)
	•	Winkies

🌟 Version B — Vocal-Heavy Cast (if you have strong singers)

Assign:
	•	High sopranos → Beauticians, Lullaby League, Crows 1, Poppies
	•	Altos/mezzos → Trees, Manicurists, Tots, Jitterbug mid
	•	Tenor/Baritone → Guard, Winkies, Polishers
	•	Non-singers → Ozians only

🌟 Version C — Dance-Heavy Cast (if they move well but don’t hold harmony)

Put your movers into:
	•	Jitterbug Chorus
	•	Ozians
	•	Beauticians
	•	Trees (comedic movement, not pure vocals)

Put your harmony-holders into:
	•	Crows
	•	Tots / Tough Kids
	•	Manicurists (top lines)
	•	Winkie Captain

⸻

🌟 Version D — Minimal Cast (10–12 performers)

	•	3 Munchkins/Beauticians/Ozians
